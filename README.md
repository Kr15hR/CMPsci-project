# Guiding Question(s)

### Where are cyclists most frequently injured and killed?

**Time Subtopic:** Are dangerous locations dangerous all the time, or just during certain hours of the day? Maybe an area isn't properly lit, or there's a prominence of drunk driving late at night.

**Cause Subtopic:** Are there common causes for crashes at the same location, or do they vary?

**Location Deeper Look:** Are there socioeconomic factors in play? Maybe underinvested neighborhoods see worse cyclist safety outcomes.
